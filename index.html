WHO WE ARE

Building a cleaner future,
one collection at a time.

Green Matter Recovery Ltd. is a waste recovery business focused on plastic and electronic waste. We work with households, schools, businesses and institutions to collect, sort and channel recyclable materials to responsible recovery partners.

Our goal is simple: make responsible waste management easier and help valuable resources find a second life.
